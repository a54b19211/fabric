Through the Years

Marvel NYC Timeline

From the first comic panels to blockbuster TV shows, New York City has always been Marvel's greatest character.

1939
Marvel Comics Founded in NYC

Timely Comics (later Marvel) was founded in Manhattan, establishing New York City as the spiritual home of the Marvel Universe from the very beginning.

1962
Spider-Man Swings Through Queens

Stan Lee and Steve Ditko introduced Spider-Man, a teenager from Forest Hills, Queens — making NYC an integral part of superhero identity for the first time.

2015
Daredevil Debuts on Netflix

Marvel's first Netflix series brought Hell's Kitchen to life with brutal realism, filming extensively on location in NYC and redefining the street-level superhero genre.

2016
Luke Cage Takes Harlem

Luke Cage celebrated Harlem's culture and history, with filming locations spanning from 125th Street to Lenox Avenue, showcasing NYC's vibrant neighborhoods.

2021
Hawkeye & Spider-Man: No Way Home

Hawkeye brought holiday cheer to Manhattan's streets while Spider-Man swung through iconic landmarks. NYC was back at the center of the MCU.

2024
Daredevil: Born Again

Matt Murdock returns to Hell's Kitchen in a new Disney+ series, with extensive filming across all five NYC boroughs, proving the city remains Marvel's greatest stage.
